Frequently asked

Do I need to sign up?
No. The app starts with an anonymous account. Link Sign in with Apple or Google in Profile to keep your archives when you change phones.
How do I share an archive?
Become friends first (Explore → search a nickname), then invite them from the archive’s edit screen. Up to 5 members.
How do I report or block?
Tap the ⋯ menu on a moment, archive or profile and choose Report or Block.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Family Archive. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. New accounts get Premium free for the first 7 days.
How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. Your data is erased; shared archives are handed over to their members.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Deletion happens immediately.

Without the app: email yt873588@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion” and your nickname (send it from the Google or Apple email linked to the account). We confirm it is your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted: your account, profile and profile photo, friends and settings, archives that have no other members (with all their photos and videos), and every photo and video you added to shared archives.

What is kept: archives shared with other members are handed over to them (without your photos and videos). Purchase and subscription records stay with Google Play, the App Store and our payment processor (RevenueCat) as required for billing and tax law. Cancel an active subscription in the store; deleting the account does not cancel it.

자주 묻는 질문

가입해야 하나요?
아니요. 익명 계정으로 바로 시작합니다. 휴대폰을 바꿔도 아카이브를 이어 쓰려면 프로필에서 Apple·Google 로그인을 연결하세요.
아카이브는 어떻게 함께 쓰나요?
먼저 친구가 되고(탐색 → 닉네임 검색) 아카이브 수정 화면에서 초대하세요. 멤버는 최대 5명입니다.
신고·차단은 어떻게 하나요?
기록·아카이브·프로필의 ⋯ 메뉴에서 신고 또는 차단을 고르세요.
구독은 어떻게 관리·해지하나요?
아이폰: 설정 → 내 이름 → 구독 → Family Archive. 안드로이드: Google Play → 결제 및 정기 결제. 새 계정은 첫 7일 동안 프리미엄을 무료로 씁니다.
계정은 어떻게 삭제하나요?
프로필 → 계정 삭제. 데이터가 지워지고, 공유하던 아카이브는 멤버에게 넘어갑니다.
