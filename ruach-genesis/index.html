Frequently asked

I can't hear any sound.
Check that your phone's silent switch is off (iPhone) or that the ringer mode is not silent or vibrate (Android), and turn the volume up. Then check the sound button in the app.
How do I change the language?
Tap the globe button.
How do I go back to the beginning?
Tap the “Start over” button.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No sign-in. You can read offline; the internet is needed only to buy or restore the unlock (the first 10 scenes are free).
How do I delete my data?
Everything is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Your purchase stays with your Apple or Google account — after reinstalling, tap “Restore purchase” on the unlock screen.
