Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Operator: Ruach · Contact: yt873588@gmail.com

Ruach Pop-up Book - Genesis (“the app”) presents Genesis as a 3D pop-up book in 62 scenes. It has no server, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. The first 10 scenes are free; one in-app purchase unlocks the rest. The app uses the internet only to talk to the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore that purchase.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else. We have no server that could receive it.

2. What stays on your device

  • Where you were reading (the scene).
  • The language you chose.

This information is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Whether sound plays follows your phone's silent/vibrate mode (on Android the app reads the ringer mode on the device only). The app uses vibration feedback, which has nothing to do with personal information.

3. In-app purchase

Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details or account information; the app only learns from the store whether the unlock has been purchased, and remembers that on your device.

4. Permissions

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library, and has no advertising identifier or cross-app tracking.

5. Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase everything it stored. Your purchase stays with your Apple or Google account; after reinstalling, tap “Restore purchase”.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date.
