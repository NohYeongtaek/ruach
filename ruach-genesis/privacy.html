Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · Operator: Ruach · Contact: yt873588@gmail.com

Ruach Pop-up Book - Genesis (“the app”) presents Genesis as a 3D pop-up book in 62 scenes. It has no server, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else. We have no server that could receive it.

2. What stays on your device

  • Where you were reading (the scene).
  • The language you chose.

This information is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Whether sound plays follows your phone's silent/vibrate mode (on Android the app reads the ringer mode on the device only). The app uses vibration feedback, which has nothing to do with personal information.

3. Permissions

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library, and has no advertising identifier or cross-app tracking.

4. Deleting your data

Delete the app to erase everything it stored. Your purchase stays with your Apple or Google account.

5. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date.
